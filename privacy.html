"Aqlli yordamchi" — bitta foydalanuvchi (ilova egasi) uchun yaratilgan shaxsiy yordamchi.

  • Ilova Google hisobiga faqat egasining ruxsati bilan ulanadi va Google Drive fayllarini faqat egasining so'rovlarini bajarish uchun o'qiydi. Yozish faqat ilovaning o'zi yaratgan fayllarga ("Jarvis natijalar" papkasi) qilinadi.
  • Ma'lumotlar uchinchi shaxslarga sotilmaydi, berilmaydi va reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi.
  • Ma'lumotlar faqat egasining serverida saqlanadi. Egasi istalgan vaqtda Google hisobi sozlamalaridan ruxsatni bekor qilishi mumkin.
  • Google API'dan olingan ma'lumotlardan foydalanish Google API Services User Data Policy, jumladan Limited Use talablariga mos keladi.

Privacy Policy (English)

"Aqlli yordamchi" is a private assistant used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to fulfil the owner's own requests, writes only to files it creates, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings.
